# Levix Plus subscription

Levix Plus is an optional paid subscription available in eligible official Android builds. Subscribing to Levix Plus does not limit, alter or remove any features available in the free version of Levix. Subscription verification is handled by Leviro's verification service at https://levix-plus.leviro.net.

Each subscription may be active on one installation at a time. You may move that subscription to another installation up to three times during any rolling 30-day period. Additional moves may be declined until the rolling period permits another move. If you replace or reset your device, this may count as an installation move. The app does not display a remaining-move count. If you need an exception to this policy, contact Leviro Support through in-app Settings > Feedback.

Subscription purchase, renewal, cancellation and refunds are processed exclusively through Google Play subject to its applicable terms. Your Plus customizations, presets and local backups are not proof of an active subscription and do not transfer purchase rights between installations or accounts.
